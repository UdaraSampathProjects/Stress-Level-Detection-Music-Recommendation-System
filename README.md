<h1>Stress Detection and Music Recommendation System 🎶🤖</h1>
<p>This project developed at <strong>SLIIT</strong> combines <strong>Artificial Intelligence</strong> (AI) and <strong>Deep Learning</strong> to offer a personalized approach to mental well-being. The system detects stress levels in real-time and provides music recommendations to help reduce stress based on emotional states derived from facial expressions and vocal tone. 😊🎵</p>

<h2>Key Features 🔑</h2>

<h3>1. Stress Level Detection 🧠</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Facial Expression Recognition:</strong>
    <p>The system utilizes the <strong>AffectNet dataset</strong> to detect stress by analyzing facial expressions. A <strong>Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)</strong> model is trained to classify images into three stress categories: <em>High</em>, <em>Medium</em>, and <em>Low</em>. The system identifies these levels based on facial emotions, such as tension (😟), calmness (🙂), and relaxation (😌).</p>
</li>

<li><strong>Voice Emotion Detection:</strong>
    <p>The <strong>TESS dataset</strong> is used to analyze voice tone and speech patterns to detect emotional states, further improving the accuracy of the stress level prediction. It works by detecting emotions expressed through vocal characteristics, such as tone, pitch, and speed, to provide a comprehensive stress assessment. 🎤📊</p>
</li>

</ul>

<h3>2. Fusion of Modalities 🔄</h3>
<p>The system uses a <strong>weighted-average fusion</strong> technique, combining the predictions from both the facial expression and voice emotion models to provide a more accurate and holistic stress assessment. ⚖️💡</p>

<h3>3. Music Recommendation 🎶</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Based on the detected stress level, the system recommends music tailored to match the user's emotional state. 🎧❤️</li>
    <li>The <strong>GTZAN dataset</strong> is used to identify and cluster songs by mood, ensuring that the music selected effectively helps to reduce stress. 🎵💆‍♂️</li>
    <li>Songs are clustered using <strong>K-means clustering</strong> to align with the identified stress level, providing users with a personalized playlist to help them relax and de-stress. 🎶🤩</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technologies Used 🛠️</h2>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Deep Learning:</strong> <em>Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)</em> for both facial expression and voice emotion detection. 🤖🧠</li>
    <li><strong>Data Processing:</strong> Data from the <em>AffectNet</em> and <em>TESS</em> datasets for training models. 📊🔍</li>
    <li><strong>Machine Learning:</strong> <em>K-means clustering</em> for grouping music by mood and stress level. 🤖🎶</li>
</ul>

<h2>Impact 🌍</h2>
<p>This project demonstrates how AI can be utilized to enhance mental well-being. By combining emotion detection with music, it offers an innovative way to manage stress and provides a scientifically-backed, real-time solution for users to regulate their emotional state. 🌟💆‍♀️</p>

<h2>Conclusion 🎯</h2>
<p>The <strong>Stress Detection and Music Recommendation System</strong> highlights how AI can be leveraged for emotional health, providing a seamless, real-time solution for managing stress through personalized music recommendations based on the user's emotional state. 🎧❤️🤖</p>
